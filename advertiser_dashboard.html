What is the Advertiser Dashboard?

The Advertiser Dashboard is a standalone module within the Mindful Publishing Intelligence platform — built to solve one specific, immediate problem: clear, accurate, advertiser-ready reporting without spreadsheets.

It brings advertising performance, content engagement, and audience data into one unified, shareable view your advertisers can access directly — in real time.

Works with your current publishing setup

You don't need to change your CMS or overhaul your tech stack. The Advertiser Dashboard works alongside your existing tools and can be implemented quickly and independently.
